Live Amazon Rank Tracker — Privacy Policy

Milton Styles LLC  ·  Effective August 27, 2026

The bottom line Rank results stay in your browser. We do not upload them. Search HTML is fetched and classified locally. Files download to your computer.

What This Extension Does

Live Amazon Rank Tracker is a Chrome extension that checks today's Amazon search for the ASINs and keywords you enter and exports the ranks to CSV or Excel.

When you click "Check ranks," the extension fetches Amazon search results in your Chrome profile for the marketplace you selected. It reads those pages in the browser, finds your ASINs, and shows placement rows (type, Rank, Absolute, page) on the app tab.

A Pro check may temporarily set Amazon Deliver-to to the zip or postcode you typed (a glow / address-change request to Amazon), then restore the previous location. Free checks do not change Deliver-to.

When you click CSV or Excel, the file downloads to your computer. Milton Styles LLC never sees, receives, or stores that file.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect any personal information
  • We do not collect your Amazon account credentials, username, or password
  • We do not collect your Amazon session tokens or cookies
  • We do not record or store your browsing history
  • We do not collect or store the rank results you check or export
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking of any kind

How Your Data Is Handled

Rank processing is local. Search HTML is fetched in your browser and classified there. Rank results are not transmitted to Milton Styles LLC or to any server we operate.

The extension saves a small amount of data in local extension storage: the form you last used (ASINs, keywords, marketplace, options), and the last run so you can export it again. A large run may use extra local storage. That data never leaves your device.

CSV and Excel files download directly to your computer using your browser's standard file download. We never see those files.

What the Extension Sends to Amazon

A check fetches Amazon search URLs for the keywords you entered. Those requests use your existing Chrome session, so a signed-in Amazon account on this browser is inherited. We do not strip cookies or force you to log out.

A Pro check may also POST a Deliver-to zip or postcode to Amazon so the search matches the location you typed, then restore the previous location. That request goes to Amazon, not to us.

Permissions Explained

The extension requests only the permissions needed to function:

downloads To save the rank export as CSV or Excel on your computer

unlimitedStorage So a large last run can stay in local extension storage without the normal size cap

Supported Amazon hosts: amazon.com, amazon.ca, amazon.co.uk, amazon.de, amazon.fr, amazon.it, amazon.es, amazon.co.jp, amazon.in, amazon.com.mx, amazon.com.au, amazon.nl, amazon.com.br.

Third Parties

Your rank data is never shared with any third party. The extension has no backend servers and no analytics service.

Payment processing (Pro tier only): If you subscribe to the Pro tier, payment processing is handled by ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) using Stripe. ExtensionPay uses a non-personally-identifiable user identifier — derived from your Chrome browser, not your Amazon account — to verify subscription status. Stripe collects and processes your payment information directly. Full payment method details — including your full card number and security code — are handled exclusively by Stripe and are never visible to Milton Styles LLC. Your name, card information, and payment data are subject to the privacy policies of ExtensionPay and Stripe.

Subscription status check: When the extension loads, it contacts extensionpay.com to verify your Pro subscription status. No rank results, Amazon account information, or browsing history is included in this request.
