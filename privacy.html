Live Amazon Rank Tracker — Privacy Policy

Milton Styles LLC  ·  Effective November 2, 2026

The bottom line We never see your rank results: checks run in your browser and files download to your computer. We only receive an email address if you subscribe to Pro, log in to it, or choose to leave one on the removal feedback page.

What This Extension Does

Live Amazon Rank Tracker is a Chrome extension that searches Amazon in your browser for the keywords you enter, finds your ASINs in today's results, and saves the ranks as CSV or Excel files on your computer.

With Pro or the Pro trial you can set a zip or postcode: the extension asks Amazon to use that delivery location for the check, confirms it on the search page, and afterwards restores your previous location when that was a local postcode (otherwise Deliver-to stays on the one you used). If Amazon shows no local delivery location, Free and Pro checks use a standard local city for that marketplace (Amazon Netherlands sets delivery by country only). These requests go to Amazon, not to us. If you are signed in to Amazon in this browser, Amazon may use that session, just as when you search yourself.

What We Do Not Collect

  • Your rank results or the files you export
  • Your Amazon login, password, session tokens or cookies
  • Your browsing history
  • Analytics, crash reports, tracking pixels or usage tracking of any kind

What Stays in Your Browser

The extension remembers your last form (ASINs, keywords, marketplace and options), your last check so you can export it again, your scheduled jobs, and the start date of your 14-day Pro trial, all on this computer. ExtensionPay, our payment service, keeps the install date and, once you open the upgrade or login page, its key for your browser and your subscription record in your browser's sync storage, which Chrome can sync to your Google account. We don't receive these browser copies; the email ExtensionPay shares with us is covered below.

Your Email Address

You can use the free version and the 14-day Pro trial without giving us an email. We receive an email address in two cases only:

  • Pro. If you subscribe or log in, you enter your email on ExtensionPay's checkout or login page, and ExtensionPay shares it with us. The extension also keeps your subscription record, including that email, in your browser's sync storage.
  • Removal feedback. If you choose to leave an email on the feedback page, we use it only to reply to you.

We use a Pro email to run your subscription: confirming it, a one-time welcome email, and replies when you write to us. The welcome email may mention our other tools. Beyond that, we send news about new features or products only if you ask for it (reply “news”), and you can stop it at any time by replying “unsubscribe”. We never sell or rent your email, never give it to advertising platforms, and never use it for ads. To have us delete it, write to support@miltonstyles.com and we will remove it from our records. ExtensionPay and Stripe may keep their own records, such as billing records the law requires.

Third Parties

  • Amazon receives the searches each check runs, as when you search Amazon yourself.
  • ExtensionPay and Stripe (Pro only) handle checkout and payment. Card details go to Stripe and are never visible to us. When the extension loads, it checks your subscription status with extensionpay.com; no rank results or Amazon account data are included. To use Pro on another computer, log in with the same email.
  • Google Forms. When you remove the extension, the browser opens an optional feedback page; Google Forms receives only the answers you submit. The page address includes the extension version and whether Pro, the Pro trial or Free was active.
